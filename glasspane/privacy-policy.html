Glasspane privacy policy

Glasspane: Always on Top & Window Tools, by PaperKite. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Glasspane doesn't collect any data. Nothing leaves your PC. There's no account, no analytics or tracking, no ads, and Glasspane never connects to the internet.

Network use

None. Glasspane works entirely offline. You buy it in the Microsoft Store, and Microsoft handles that payment under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Glasspane never sees your payment details.

What Glasspane looks at to work

To pin, fade, move and arrange your windows, Glasspane reads the titles, positions and program names of the windows open on your PC. It uses them only while it runs, on your PC, and never records or shares them.

  • Mini window and dark mode show the live picture of a window you choose, on your own screen only. Nothing is saved or recorded.
  • Mute any app uses Windows' own volume switch for that app (the same one as the volume mixer). Glasspane never records or listens to any sound.
  • Keyboard shortcuts are registered with Windows, which tells Glasspane only when one of its own shortcuts is pressed. Glasspane doesn't see or log anything else you type.
  • Drag anywhere (off unless you turn it on) watches for a mouse press while you hold Alt or the Windows key, so it can move the window under the pointer. Nothing is recorded.

What stays on your PC

Glasspane keeps these in its own folder on your PC: your settings and shortcuts, saved layouts and app rules, where your windows were for each monitor setup, which apps you muted with Glasspane, a small safety record of the changes it made to windows (so it can always put them back, even after a crash), and a local log of errors. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling Glasspane removes them.

Other apps

If you turn on "Dim during Dumbtop focus sessions" and Dumbtop (by the same developer) is installed, Glasspane reads Dumbtop's timer file on your PC to know when a focus session is running. Dumbtop can also tell Glasspane directly when a session starts or stops. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Children

Glasspane collects no information from anyone, including children.
