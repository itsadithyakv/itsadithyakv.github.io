Glasspane privacy policy

Glasspane: Always on Top & Pin Windows, by PaperKite. Last updated 23 November 2026 (version 1.2.0).

The Glasspane app doesn't collect any data. It never sends anything about you or your windows. There's no Glasspane account, no analytics or tracking and no ads. The app only connects to the Microsoft Store, to sell and confirm Glasspane Pro. The developer receives something from you only if you choose to write.

Network use

Glasspane works offline. Its only connection is the Microsoft Store's own purchase system. Glasspane uses it to show Pro's price, to buy or restore Pro, and, a little after it starts and then once a day, to ask whether the Microsoft Store account on your PC owns Pro. Glasspane adds nothing about you, your windows or how you use it to these requests. Microsoft handles them, and any payment, under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Glasspane never sees your payment details. Once confirmed, Pro is remembered on your PC, so it keeps working offline.

What Glasspane looks at to work

All of this happens on your PC, and none of it is sent anywhere.

  • Your windows: to find, pin, fade, move and arrange them, Glasspane reads their titles, icons, positions, app names and program locations, which window is in front, and whether something is full screen. It also reads how your monitors are arranged.
  • The screen behind its glass: to draw its glass, Glasspane reads the area of the screen behind and just around its bubbles, messages and change notes, and refreshes it while they show. That picture stays in memory and is never saved.
  • Mini windows, bubble cards, mirrors to another screen and dark mode show the picture of a window on your own screens. For mini windows, bubble cards and mirrors, Windows draws that picture and Glasspane doesn't read it. Dark mode reads it to make it dark. It is never saved.
  • "Tell me when it changes" (Glasspane Pro), only for a window you choose: Windows gives Glasspane the picture of that window, and Glasspane keeps only a small copy of the part you picked, about every 0.7 seconds, to compare and notice a change. The copies stay in memory while it watches, the latest one is shown in the note it gives you, and none is ever saved.
  • Sound and media: to mute apps, change an app's volume and mark the bubble that's playing, Glasspane reads which apps are playing sound and their volume. From Windows' media controls it reads the title and artist of what's playing in a window, to show them on that window's bubble card, and presses play, pause or skip for that window only when you ask. It never records or listens to the sound, and doesn't save those titles.
  • The pointer: Glasspane reads where the pointer is, to show its buttons and bubble cards, to fade bubbles, to show a dot where it is on a mirror and to pass your clicks and scrolling from a mini window to the real window. It keeps no history of it.
  • Optional mouse shortcuts (Ctrl + scroll, Shift + double-click and drag anywhere): while any of them is on, Glasspane watches mouse clicks, movement and scrolling to recognise them. Nothing is recorded.
  • Keys: keyboard shortcuts are registered with Windows, which tells Glasspane only when one of them is pressed. For mouse gestures it also checks whether Ctrl, Shift, Alt or the Windows key is held, and it checks for Esc while you pick an area of a window. It doesn't see or log what you type in other apps.

What stays on your PC

Glasspane keeps these in its own folder on your PC, and never sends them anywhere:

  • your settings, shortcuts and chosen apps, and whether you own Pro;
  • saved layouts, app rules and where windows were for each monitor setup, which can include window titles, app names and program locations;
  • a safety record of the changes it made to windows, so it can always put them back, even after a crash, which includes those windows' titles and app names, a list of apps it muted, and the volume of each app it changed (with that app's program location), so it can be put back;
  • a small local log of events and errors, which can include app names, layout names and window titles.

Uninstalling Glasspane removes them.

If you email the developer

"Bought Glasspane before it was free?" on the Pro page opens your own email app with a message filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you write, the developer receives your email address and whatever you include, such as a Microsoft Store order date or number, and uses it only to answer you (for example, to check the order and send you a code for Glasspane Pro). Your email provider and the developer's (Google) handle the message.

Other apps

Glasspane checks whether Framecap and Dumbtop (by the same developer) are installed, to offer features that work with them. If you turn on "Dim during Dumbtop focus sessions", it reads Dumbtop's timer file on your PC to know when a focus session is running. Dumbtop can also tell Glasspane directly when a session starts or stops. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Children

The Glasspane app collects no information from anyone, including children.
